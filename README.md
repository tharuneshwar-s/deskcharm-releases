## What is DeskCharm?

A **charm** (a nazar, a temple bell, Ganesha, a lucky elephant…) hangs on a string from the top corner of your screen. It swings when you move past it; you can grab it, fling it, squeeze it and slide its beads. Now and then a **desk visitor** walks along your taskbar, does a little act and reminds you to drink water, breathe or take a break. Gentle **calm tools** and **insights** help you through the working day.

The charm lives in a see-through window: clicks pass through to whatever is underneath. Only the charm, visitors and their buttons catch the mouse.

## Features

### 🧿 Charms
- Charm packs: **Nazar**, **Indian Gods**, Friends, Zodiac, Birthstones, Crystals, Temple, Lucky and more.
- Living eyes and faces, realistic swing physics, touch, fling and squeeze.
- **One or two charms** on screen, with matching **themes**.
- Add your own charm from any PNG.

### 🐘 Desk visitors
- A cat, a baby elephant, **Vijay the water buddy** and more, walking along your taskbar.
- **Festival visitors** for Diwali, Pongal, Christmas and other days we celebrate.
- Visitors can carry your reminders: choose **who visits for what** (Auto, Favourite or Custom).
- They stay away during full-screen apps, games and meetings.

## Private by design

Your settings, tasks, reminders and insights stay **on your PC**. No account, no analytics, no tracking.
DeskCharm only goes online to check for updates (this repository), and for live weather or calendar quiet if you turn them on.
Pro licence keys are checked **offline**.

## Install

1. Download `DeskCharm_<version>_x64-setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/tharuneshwar-s/deskcharm-releases/releases/latest).
2. Run it. It installs for your user only; no administrator rights needed.

> **Windows protected your PC?** The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click **More info › Run anyway**. To verify the file, compare `Get-FileHash .\DeskCharm_*_x64-setup.exe` in PowerShell with the SHA-256 on the release page.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Uninstall from **Settings › Apps**.
