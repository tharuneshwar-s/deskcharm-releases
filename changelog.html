1.8.0

29 October 2026

New

  • DeskCharm has a home: deskcharm.in. Buy Pro there (₹69 launch, then ₹99), recover a lost key on Find my key, and email support@deskcharm.in for help. Downloads and updates now come from the deskcharm-releases page on GitHub. Settings › About has an Email support link.
  • Who visits for what. A new card on the Visitors page decides which visitor comes for water, stretch, posture, breaks, eye rest, reminders, focus nudges, mood cheer, the morning hello and counters. Auto sends the best fit most of the time and another of your visitors now and then; Favourite sends one visitor for everything; Custom (Pro) lets you pick per job. Every row has Try it.
  • Any visitor can do any job. The cat can bring your water question, the elephant can ask you to stretch: YES still counts the glass, Done still logs the stretch. Vijay, the yogi, the chai-wala and the myna keep their own acts.
  • Your own words (Pro). A custom line per job, and hello / goodbye lines for each visitor, shown exactly as you type them.
  • Counters. Settings › Calm › Counters: count anything (push-ups, prayers, pages…) with a name, emoji and daily goal. A visitor asks “name? emoji” every so often within your hours; YES adds one. Up to three small rings beside the charm (water plus two counters), and counters in Insights and This week. One counter free, unlimited with Pro.
  • Track your routines. Tick Track it on a reminder: each time it is done or missed is recorded, with a streak of days you did them all. New Insights card Your routines and a This week row. Three tracked reminders free, unlimited with Pro.

Fixed

  • Visitors are no longer cut off: the cat's stretch, the elephant's and monkey's tails, the Holi runner's back foot, the Diwali kid setting down his lamp and the myna's wing are drawn whole, and wide visitors like the peacock now walk in and out fully beyond the screen edge.

1.6.0

26 October 2026

New

  • Mood check-in. Once a day (from a time you choose) the charm asks "How are you feeling?" with five faces. Click one, or log any time from right-click › How do you feel?. On a rough day it offers a minute of breathing and a visitor comes by to cheer you up. Insights shows a 7-day mood strip and this week's mood against last week.
  • Themes. Ten ready-made looks that set both charms, their rope and beads, an ambience sound and a soft glow in one click: Temple morning, Rainy focus, Ocean calm, Cosmic (your zodiac sign + birthstone from your birthday), Toro & Kuro, Diwali glow, Garden, Midnight, Pongal and more.
  • 16 new Friends charms: Kuro (Toro's buddy, with a living face), Baby Ganesha, Peachick, Temple Monkey, Temple Cow, Shiba, Panda, Bunny, Penguin, Axolotl, and Tamil cinema minis: Vijay, Rajini, Ajith, Suriya, Kamal and Dhanush.
  • One or two charms. Choose One or Two at the top of the Charm page (or tick right-click › Two charms). Charm 2 gets its own tab in the picker, its own size and string length, and hangs beside the first (close enough to clink) or from the opposite top corner. Both breathe, sway, glow and can be flung; living faces on both follow your cursor.

Improved

  • The cat, elephant, peacock, monkey and myna were redrawn so every walking and acting frame matches. The myna now flies level, and the monkey holds the borrowed bead properly.

1.5.0

24 October 2026

New

  • Focus tasks. Starting a focus session asks what you're working on (type it or pick from your list). The charm shows the task, asks "Done?" at the break, and Insights lists what you finished. Right-click › Tasks for quick access.
  • Reminders. Your own reminders: every day, on chosen weekdays, every few minutes or hours within a time window, or once. Each one arrives as a charm cue with a chime, a visitor of your choice who asks with Done / Later, and a Windows notification. Settings › Calm › Your reminders.
  • This week on Insights: focus, breathing, breaks, eye rests, water and tasks compared with last week, plus your streak and best day.
  • Choose your screen (Settings › General). Falls back to the main screen if yours is unplugged and comes back when it returns.
  • New charms: Zodiac (12), Birthstones (12), Crystals & gems (8) and Temple (8).
  • Report a problem (Settings › General › Help) opens a prefilled GitHub issue. Your name, calendar link, intention and app list are never included.

Improved

  • Less memory: the Settings window is only created when you open it, and fully closed 5 minutes after you close it.

1.4.0

24 October 2026

New

  • Toro charm. The music-loving cat in big headphones hangs as a charm in the new Friends category. His face follows your cursor, dozes off when you're away, gets dizzy after a hard fling and bobs to your music.
  • Lifelike visitors. Every desk visitor (the elephant, cat, peacock, monkey, yogi, chai-wala, myna and the festival visitors) is now a realistic figure that walks, rides or flies with real animation.
  • Living charm. On Classic Blue, Gold, Emerald and Hamsa Hand the eye follows your cursor, blinks, smiles when you play, gets dizzy after a hard fling and dozes off when you're away. Turn it off in Settings › Play.
  • Water ring and stats. A small ring by the charm shows today's glasses against your goal. Insights now counts water too, with water bars in the 7-day chart. When you say NO to Vijay, choose when he comes back: 15 min, 30 min or 1 hour.
  • Ambient sounds. Rain, wind, ocean, temple bells or wind chimes, made live on your computer, with no recordings. They can play by themselves during breathing and focus sessions. Right-click the charm › Ambience, or Settings › Calm.
  • Daily ritual. A gentle greeting each morning, today's intention shown when you rest your cursor on the charm, an optional evening wind-down, and a short weekly look back on Sunday evening.
  • Stretch and posture nudges. The yogi comes by to stretch with you or remind you to sit tall, on an interval you choose.
  • Focus guard (opt-in). During a focus session, if you open an app on your distraction list, a visitor nudges you back. The app check runs only on your computer and nothing is stored.
  • Meeting-aware quiet. Visitors stay away while your microphone is in use, and optionally while your calendar (a private .ics link) says you're busy.
  • More shortcuts. Set global keys to send a visitor, count a glass of water or start a breathing minute (Settings › General).
  • Backup and restore. Export your settings and custom charms to one file and bring them back on another PC.
  • தமிழ். The charm and visitors can speak Tamil (Settings › General › Language).
  • First-run tips show new users how to fling, rest on and right-click the charm.

Improved

  • Much lower CPU use when the charm is still: the overlay rests until something moves.
  • A local error log (Settings › General › Open log folder) to help with bug reports. Nothing is ever sent.
  • Reminders no longer interrupt each other or Vijay's question, and cues never overlap or get cut off at the screen edge.

1.3.0

23 October 2026

  • Automatic updates. DeskCharm checks for new versions in the background, tells you when one is ready, and installs it with one click — only when you say so. Updates are signed and verified.
  • New Settings. Seven clear pages instead of one long scroll, a live charm preview you can play with, changes that save as you make them, quick buttons to start breathing or focus, and reset links.
  • Snappier dragging. The charm stays exactly under your cursor while you drag (no more floating behind or shaking), keeps the spot you grabbed instead of twisting on grab, flings with the speed of your hand, and is easier to catch on the move. Motion is smooth on any monitor refresh rate.
  • Desk visitors. Now and then a baby elephant, a cat, a peacock, a cheeky monkey (who borrows a bead from your charm — click him to get it back) or a floating yogi walks along the top of your taskbar and does a little trick; click one to see it again. On festival days a Diwali lamp kid, a Holi colour runner, a Pongal pot carrier or a reindeer joins them. They wave when your cursor passes, hop to the beat with music sway on, run off if you fling the charm at them, and the first one each day says good morning. With reminders on, a chai-wala cycles in for your break and a myna lands for eye rest; click the yogi to start a breathing session. A new Visitors page in Settings shows a visitor of the day, a preview stage and a gallery of every visitor; give each one its own look, size, trick speed, direction, times of day, sounds, speech bubbles, charm tricks, reactions, name and greeting, and see today’s visits. Send or pause them from the right-click menu.
  • Water reminder. Every hour (you choose), Vijay walks in, greets you with a “Vanakkam nanba! 🙏” and asks you to drink some water. Tap YES and he cheers “Verithanam 🔥” and counts a glass; tap NO and he gets sad and asks again later. Set the interval, your daily goal and the name he calls you in Settings › Calm, or press Try it now.
  • Touchscreens: a tap grabs the charm right away.

1.2.0

16 October 2026

New

  • Insights: today's focus and breathing minutes, breaks and eye rests, a 7-day chart and your calm streak.
  • App usage (opt-in): time per app and app switches per hour, kept for 30 days on your computer; a gentle nudge to breathe when you switch apps a lot.

  • macOS and Linux: first beta builds.

Changed

  • Opening DeskCharm while it's already running now opens Settings.

1.1.0

13 October 2026

New

  • Drag anywhere: grab the charm and stretch the rope like a rubber band; let go and it flings back.
  • Fidget toys: squeeze the charm, slide the beads, wind the rope around its hook, click fast for a sparkle burst, and hang a second charm that clacks against the first.
  • Melody chimes: swings play soft notes from a calm scale.
  • Breathe & focus: guided breathing (1, 3 or 5 min), a 25/5 focus timer, break reminders and 20-20-20 eye rest.
  • Breathing glow: rest your cursor on the charm and it glows in a slow breathing rhythm.
  • Reacts to your PC: sway to music, typing breeze and window shake.
  • Seasons: festival decorations (Diwali, Navratri, Holi, Pongal, Eid, Christmas, New Year), a warmer mood at night, and optional live weather wind.
  • Your own charm: add any PNG or WebP as a charm.
  • Right-click menu on the charm: Settings, Hide, Mute.

Changed

  • Settings closes after saving; closing without saving undoes the preview.
  • Left-clicking the tray icon opens Settings.

Fixed

  • Saved settings now load reliably at startup.
  • The tray's Show/Hide label stays in sync with the hotkey.
  • Only one copy of DeskCharm can run at a time.
  • Start with Windows works for usernames with spaces.
  • A damaged settings file no longer resets everything.
