# DeskCharm

A little charm for your desktop that keeps you calm, watered and smiling. Windows · v1.6.0 · free beta

## What it is
A physics charm (nazar, temple bell, Ganesha and more) hangs on your screen. Drag, fling and squeeze it. Friendly visitors drop by, like **Vijay the water buddy**, and festivals (Diwali, Holi, Pongal, Christmas) decorate your desk on the day.

## Why it matters
Desk workers sit for hours and forget water, breaks and breathing. Most break apps are cold timers that get muted. DeskCharm leads with **culture and delight**, so people keep it on, and then quietly helps with **breaks, posture, water, breathing, mood, focus and reminders**, staying quiet during meetings. Insights show your week kindly.

## Private by design
Everything stays on your PC. No account, no tracking. Only exceptions: the update check, and opt-in weather and calendar.

## Model
- **Free:** classic charms (Nazar, Gods), all visitors, all calm tools.
- **Pro:** lifetime licence, 7-day trial. ₹69 launch / ₹99 regular, sold on deskcharm.in (Razorpay; cards from outside India coming soon). All charm packs, unlimited custom charms, two charms, full Insights history.
- **Teams:** free 30-day pilot for 3–5 companies, measured with a self-reported anonymous survey. Pricing after pilots.
