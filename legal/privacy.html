Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-01 · effective 1 November 2026

DeskCharm is made and sold by Tharuneshwar S, an individual in Puducherry, India ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

The app stays offline

The app has no account and sends no analytics or usage data. Your settings, charms, moods and Insights stay on your PC. Your licence key is checked on your PC, offline. The app only goes online to:

  • check GitHub for updates (you can turn this off);
  • fetch weather or read your calendar, only if you turn those features on.

What we collect when you buy Pro

  • Name, email address and phone number (all required at checkout).
  • Payment ids: the Razorpay order id and payment id, the amount and, when Razorpay provides it, the card's country. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles them.
  • Your licence key and its id.
  • Your consents: whether you ticked email updates and WhatsApp/SMS updates, and the version of this policy you saw.
  • Whether you came from the website or the app.

Why we use it

  • Your purchase: creating the order, issuing your key and handling refunds.
  • Support and key recovery: finding your key if you lose it, and answering your emails.
  • Receipts: emailing your key and receipt.
  • Updates by email: only if you ticked the box. Every such email has an unsubscribe line.
  • Updates by WhatsApp/SMS: only if you ticked the box.
  • Future services linked to your licence: if we offer new services tied to your Pro licence, we'll describe them here first and ask again if they need new data.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone except the processors below.

Who processes it for us

  • Razorpay: payments and refunds.
  • Supabase: the database holding purchase records.
  • Resend: sending key and receipt emails from support@deskcharm.in.
  • Cloudflare: our checkout server (api.deskcharm.in), DNS and email routing.
  • GitHub: hosting this website, the downloads and the app's update check.

How long we keep it

We keep purchase records for as long as your licence exists (it's lifetime, so you can always recover your key), and at least as long as Indian tax law requires. If you ask us to delete your record, we delete what we're not legally required to keep; after that we can no longer re-send your key, but the key keeps working. Opt-in consents are kept until you withdraw them.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to see what we hold about you, correct or update it, have it deleted, withdraw consent for updates at any time, and raise a grievance. Email support@deskcharm.in from the address you bought with; we reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the version above. If a change needs new data or a new use, we ask you first.
